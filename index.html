Blank Mind Logical Protocol (BMLP)

An original research methodology, developed independently by the proprietor, for stress-testing AI systems and examining structural contradictions in established reasoning frameworks. Applied by the author across six deployed AI systems and documented in four DOI-registered working papers.

  • Recursive, builder-independent interrogation method
  • Structural-contradiction and consistency testing
  • Applied across Claude, Gemini, ChatGPT, Copilot, Mistral, Meta AI

Independent research. Findings are reported from the author's own sessions and have not yet been independently replicated or reviewed by a regulatory body or academic institution.
